# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Reports can be made privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
